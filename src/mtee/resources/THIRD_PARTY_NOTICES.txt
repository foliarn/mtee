distributed under the BSD-3-Clause license; its metadata also lists

bundled next to this file in resources/licenses/numpy/LICENSE.txt.

libmpv Windows x86-64
---------------------
MTEE bundles libmpv-2.dll from the zhongfly/mpv-winbuild project.

Provider: https://github.com/zhongfly/mpv-winbuild
Release: 2026-09-23-6fd80b2003
Archive: mpv-dev-lgpl-x86_64-20260923-git-6fd80b2003.7z
Archive SHA-256: 8d65bab888f5dfff059c50431d2c72c4c6ab78706c363056697176694563321d
DLL SHA-256: 85071ee7c8bfc9f3690a0eb140f15bbb697cdf81bc96e9c905dc8a54cfff8c10d

The provider describes this build as libmpv under LGPL-2.1-or-later with
FFmpeg statically linked under LGPL-3.0-or-later. The provider's Windows build
scripts are licensed under MIT. License texts and source are available at:

  mpv: https://github.com/mpv-player/mpv
  FFmpeg: https://github.com/FFmpeg/FFmpeg
  Build scripts: https://github.com/zhongfly/mpv-winbuild
  LGPL-2.1: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.txt
  LGPL-3.0: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/lgpl-3.0.txt
  MIT: https://opensource.org/license/mit

The builder cautions that it cannot guarantee all LGPL-incompatible packages
were disabled. MTEE has not completed a transitive dependency or legal audit
of this binary. See docs/native-dependencies.md in the source distribution
for the observed dependencies, provenance, and remaining redistribution work.

No ffmpeg.exe or ffprobe.exe is included. The FFmpeg libraries described above
are statically linked inside the bundled libmpv runtime.

BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds.

Provider: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
Release: autobuild-2026-09-24-14-14
FFmpeg version: n9.0.2-3-ga5923073bf-20260924
Archive: ffmpeg-n9.0.2-3-ga5923073bf-win64-lgpl-shared-9.0.zip
Archive SHA-256: 735bae484ba2c3342bfb34df477b9c6b0f43f9819f4d2fde011be293ee1b6517

Build scripts: https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds
The exact archive manifest, file hashes and acquisition procedure are in
resources/native/ffmpeg/manifest.json and scripts/fetch_ffmpeg.ps1.
